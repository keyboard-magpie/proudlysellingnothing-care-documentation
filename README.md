# Voron Design Dye-Sublimated Shirt Care Manual

> **Note on Fabric & Print:** Dye-sublimation on cotton/blend fabrics creates a vibrant, soft-feel print that fuses into the fibers rather than sitting on top like traditional screen printing. Following these guidelines ensures high color fidelity and fabric durability over time.

---

## 🛠️ Quick Reference / Quick Code

| Parameter | Setting / Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wash Temp** | `Cold` ($30^\circ\text{C}$ / $86^\circ\text{F}$ or below) |
| **Cycle** | Gentle / Delicates |
| **Detergent** | Mild, non-bleach |
| **Drying** | Air Dry / Line Dry (Preferred) |
| **Ironing** | Low heat, inside-out (no steam on print) |

---

## 🧼 Washing Instructions

1. **Invert before Washing**
   * Turn the shirt inside-out to protect the sublimated surface from mechanical abrasion against zipper teeth, buttons, or rough fabrics that are in the wash with your VORON t-shirt.

2. **Temperature Control**
   * Use cold water (30C). High water temperatures can cause microscopic fiber breakdown in cotton blends and dull dye pigments over multiple cycles.

3. **Detergent Selection**
   * Use mild liquid detergent.
   * **Avoid:** Bleach, fabric softeners, and harsh stain removers containing optical brighteners or harsh solvents. Fabric softeners leave a film that can mute print vibrancy.

4. **Load Management**
   * Wash with like colors and soft garments. Avoid washing alongside heavy denim, hook-and-loop fasteners (Velcro), or heavy workwear.

---

## 🌬️ Drying Instructions

* **Primary Method (Recommended): Air Dry**
  * Hang dry or lay flat out of direct sunlight. Direct UV exposure during drying can cause gradual color degradation.

* **Alternative Primary method**
  * Set printer heatbed to 40-50C and lay roughly folded shirt on heatbed for 45-60 minutes. Observe VERY carefully to avoid housefires.

* **Secondary Method: Tumble Dry Low**
  * If using a dryer, set it to the **Delicate / Low Heat** setting. Remove the garment immediately once the cycle completes to reduce wrinkling.

---

## 👕 Maintenance & Storage

* **Ironing & Steaming:**
  * Iron inside-out on the lowest setting if necessary. 
  * Do not iron directly on the printed graphic without a teflon or protective press cloth.
* **Storage:**
  * Store folded in a dry drawer or on padded hangers away from direct sunlight.
* **Wearing:**
  * Ensure wearage to all RRF events no matter how faded it has become.
